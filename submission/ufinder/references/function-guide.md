# Function guide

Example requests, and what each function does with them.

## Find things

**Find**
- "Find the photo of a man in a UGREEN T-shirt."
- "Where is the contract that mentions a late payment fee?"
- "I recorded something about the temple, where is it?"

**Relate**
- "What else is connected to this invoice?"
- "Show me files related to this photo."

**Moments**
- "Show me everything from our June holiday."
- "What do we have from the week of the product launch?"

**Dossier**
- "Give me everything about Project Falcon."
- "Pull together what we have on Harbor Mills."

**Missing**
- "Which files do my notes mention that I do not have?"
- "Check the Contracts folder for missing attachments."

## Understand your files

**Brief**
- "What do my files say about the rent increase?"
- "Who approved the vent work?"

**Expenses**
- "Add up the receipts from April."
- "List all invoices from Harbor Mills with totals."
- "Match these invoices to this purchase order."

**Contracts**
- "When do my contracts renew?"
- "What is the notice period on the lease?"

**Recordings**
- "Turn this recording into meeting notes."
- "What did we decide in the supplier call?"

**Digest**
- "What changed in the Bakery folder this week?"
- "What are the biggest new files this month?"

**Events**
- "What happened at the front door on Tuesday afternoon?"
- "Show camera events and any photos from that morning."

## Organize

**Albums**
- "Make an album of the team day photos with the blue shirts."
- "Create an album from the Penang holiday."

**Duplicates**
- "Find duplicates in my Photos folder."
- "How much space could I save?"

**Tidy**
- "Organize my Downloads folder by type."

**Map**
- "Map how the files in this project connect."

**Tag**
- "Tag those files as tax-2025."

**Status**
- "Is everything ready?"
- "Which AI features are working?"

## Chaining

Each result can feed the next function. Keep real paths from step to step.

- Find, then Albums: "Find the beach photos, then make an album."
- Dossier, then Tag: "Gather Project Falcon, then tag those files."
- Moments, then Brief: "Show the trip, then tell me what the hotel cost."
- Duplicates, then Tidy: "Show the duplicates, then plan the cleanup."
